Instrumentation & Measurement Component Tester

Overview

I worked with a small team in Spring 2026 to build and validate a functionality tester for 11 sensors with various communication protocols. The sensors interface with an Arduino Mega via a soldered perfboard and shield, as shown in the picture above. I developed and debugged the embedded software in C++, then integrated it with the hardware and validated the functionality of both hardware and software components. The end result reduced the time required to test a full batch of sensors by 98%.





Sensors Tested

The sensors tested had I²C, SPI, Analog, and Digital communication protocols.





Final Product

The device is used to diagnose broken sensors in the Instrumentation and Measurement class (ENGR 373) to reduce troubleshooting time during labs.
